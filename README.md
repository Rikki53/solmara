# SOLMARA — Sun, Streets & Second Chances

[![make.fun gta6-01 bounty](https://img.shields.io/badge/make.fun-gta6-01%20bounty-ffbe53?style=flat-square)](https://make.fun/c/gta6-01)

**Solmara** is an original open-world crime game built from scratch for the **make.fun gta6-01 ("Make GTA 6")** bounty. Set in a sun-soaked fictional coastal city, you play as Kai Marlow, navigating the streets on foot or behind the wheel of stolen vehicles, evading the police wanted system, and progressing through an immersive 5-mission coastal crime story.

## Features
- **Open-World Coastal City:** Procedurally generated districts (Old Marina, Palm Heights, Dockside, Coral Avenue, Sunset Hills) with glowing sunset lighting, beaches, palm trees, and traffic/pedestrian AI.
- **On-Foot & Vehicle Gameplay:** Seamlessly walk, sprint, jump, punch, or steal and drive various vehicles (sports cars, sedans, taxis, pickups) with arcade physics and damage models.
- **Wanted Level & Police AI:** 1–5 star wanted system with pursuing police cruisers, foot officers, line-of-sight detection, and Pay & Spray garages.
- **5 Story Missions:** Multi-stage narrative progression from introductory wheel-stealing to the final Bay Heist.
- **Immersive HUD & Minimap:** Top-down radar minimap, speedometer, health bar, wanted stars, cash counter, and audio mute toggle.
- **Offline First:** Runs entirely offline in any modern desktop browser with zero network requests.

## How to Play
1. Serve the `game/` folder locally or open `game/index.html` directly in desktop Chrome/Firefox/Edge.
2. Click **START GAME** or press **Enter** from the title screen.

### Controls
- **WASD / Arrow Keys**: Move character or drive vehicles
- **Shift**: Sprint / Driving Nitro
- **Space**: Jump (on foot) / Handbrake (driving)
- **E**: Enter / Exit vehicle
- **F**: Melee punch
- **M**: Toggle audio mute
- **P / Esc**: Pause menu / Quick save
- **Mouse**: Orbit camera
